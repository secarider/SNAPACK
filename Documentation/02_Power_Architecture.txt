SNAPACK Hardware Specification

Revision: Draft 1

============================================================
PURPOSE
============================================================

This Specification Defines The SNAPACK Power Distribution
Architecture.

It Documents The Battery Configuration, Power Conversion,
Voltage Rails, Isolation Boundaries, And Distribution Of
Electrical Power Throughout The System.

Connector Pin Assignments Are Defined Separately In:

    01_Authoritative_Pin_Mapping.txt

============================================================
BATTERY ARCHITECTURE
============================================================

Battery Chemistry

    LiFePO4

Cell Type

    Headway 38120HP

Configuration

    Five Cells In Series
    Five Cells In Parallel

    (5S5P)

Nominal Pack Voltage

    Approximately 16 Volts

The 4S Point Of The Pack Provides The Normal 12-Volt Output.

The 5S Pack Provides The 16-Volt Boost Output.

============================================================
PRIMARY POWER RAILS
============================================================

The SNAPACK Contains The Following Power Rails:

Modules supplied are 9 to 32 volt input and will be powered from full pack voltage 16V

• 16-Volt 5S Full Pack Voltage

• 12-Volt 4S Pack Voltage

• Regulated 5-Volt Rail

• Regulated 3.3-Volt Logic Rail

• Regulated 9V-Volt Rail
  Used By The OTC Current Sensor.

============================================================
POWER CONVERSION
============================================================

5-Volt Rail

The Primary 5-Volt Rail Powers:

• Display Assembly

• Voltage Divider Isolation Relay Module

• AMS1117 5v to 3.3v Regulator

• Other 5V Peripheral Devices

------------------------------------------------------------

3.3-Volt Rail

The System 3.3V Rail Is Generated By An AMS1117-3.3
Voltage Regulator Module Powered From The Existing
5V Rail.

The 3.3V Rail Powers:

• DS2484 I2C-to-1-Wire bridge operational at 0x18.

• DS18B20 Temperature Sensors

• ADS1115 Modules

• Future 3.3V Logic Inputs

============================================================
POWER DISTRIBUTION
============================================================

Battery Power Is Distributed According To Function.

High Current Loads

• EV200 Contactors

• LEV100 Contactors

• Output Terminals

Medium Current Loads

• DC-DC Converters

• Relay Coil Circuits

Low Power Electronics

• Display

• ADS1115 Boards

• Temperature Sensors

• Relays And Relay Module

============================================================
DESIGN REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

The 9V, 5V, And 3.3V Rails Shall Remain Stable During
Contactor Switching Events.

Logic Electronics Shall Never Operate Directly From
Battery Voltage.

All Power Conversion Modules Shall Be Mounted To
Permit Replacement Without Redesign Of Adjacent
Subsystems.
