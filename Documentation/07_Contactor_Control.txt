============================================================
PURPOSE
============================================================

This Specification Defines The High Current
Switching Hardware Used By SNAPACK.

It Covers:

• EV200 Contactors
• LEV100 Contactors
• Relay Authorization
• Auxiliary Contacts
• 12V Operation
• 16V Boost Operation

Electrical Connections Are Defined In:

    01_Authoritative_Pin_Mapping.txt

============================================================
SYSTEM OVERVIEW
============================================================

SNAPACK Uses Two Independent Contactor Systems.

EV200 Contactors

Provide Main Battery Connection.

LEV100 Contactors

Select Between Normal 12V Operation And
16V Boost Operation.

============================================================
AUTHORIZATION
============================================================

The Positive Authorization Circuit Controls
Whether The Contactors May Be Energized.

Authorization Is Removed During A Hardware
Protection Event.

The Authorization Circuit Is Interrupted By
The Opto-Isolated Relay Module.

============================================================
AUXILIARY CONTACTS
============================================================

LEV100 Auxiliary Contacts May Be Connected
To The Controller For Independent Confirmation
Of Contactor State.

Auxiliary Contacts Are Intended As Status
Feedback Only.

============================================================
MECHANICAL INSTALLATION
============================================================

Contactors Shall Be Mounted Securely To The
Main Chassis.

Bus Bars Shall Be Properly Supported.

Power Cable Routing Shall Minimize Mechanical
Stress On Contactor Terminals.

============================================================
MEASUREMENT PURPOSE
============================================================

The Contactor System Provides

• Main Pack Connection

• 12V Output Selection

• 16V Boost Selection

• Hardware Isolation

• Status Feedback
