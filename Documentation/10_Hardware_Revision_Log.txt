REVISION C
==========

ADS1115 Analog System Changed From 5V To 3.3V Operation

The ADS1115 Boards Now Operate Directly From The
Regulated 3.3V Logic Rail.

Affected Specifications

01_Authoritative_Pin_Mapping.txt

02_Power_Architecture.txt

03_Display_Interface.txt

04_ADS1115_Analog_Front_End.txt

---

I2C Level Shifter Removed

The ADS1115 Analog System And Display I2C Interface
Now Operate In The Same 3.3V Logic Domain.

The Previously Required I2C Level Shifter Is No Longer
Part Of The SNAPACK Hardware Architecture.

Affected Specifications

01_Authoritative_Pin_Mapping.txt

02_Power_Architecture.txt

03_Display_Interface.txt

04_ADS1115_Analog_Front_End.txt

---

Three ADS1115 Board Architecture Established

Three ADS1115 Boards Are Used To Provide The Required
Analog Measurement Channels.

The Third ADS1115 Provides Channels For Final Output
Voltage Monitoring And Additional Expansion Capacity.

Affected Specifications

01_Authoritative_Pin_Mapping.txt

03_Display_Interface.txt

04_ADS1115_Analog_Front_End.txt

---

Voltage Divider Architecture Revised

The Previous Common Divider Arrangement Was Revised
For Operation With The 3.3V ADS1115 Analog System.

Current Divider Architecture:

B1 = 47k High / 18k Low

B2 = 47k High / 18k Low

B3 = 47k High / 18k Low

B4 = 100k High / 10k Low

B5 = 100k High / 10k Low

24V Rail Monitor = 100k High / 10k Low

The First Three Cumulative Battery Taps Retain The
Previously Proven 47k / 18k Divider Network.

The Higher Voltage Measurement Channels Use The
100k / 10k Divider Network To Maintain Suitable
ADS1115 Input Voltage At Their Maximum Design Voltage.

Affected Specifications

01_Authoritative_Pin_Mapping.txt

04_ADS1115_Analog_Front_End.txt

---

DS2484 I2C-To-1-Wire Interface Added

The Temperature Sensor Interface Now Uses A DS2484
I2C-To-1-Wire Bridge.

The DS2484 Operates From The Regulated 3.3V Rail
And Communicates With The ESP32 Through The Shared
3.3V I2C Bus.

The DS18B20 Temperature Sensors Operate On The
OneWire Bus Downstream Of The DS2484.

The Previous Direct ESP32 OneWire Temperature
Connection Is No Longer Used.

Affected Specifications

02_Power_Architecture.txt

03_Display_Interface.txt

06_Temperature_Monitoring.txt

---

ADS1115 ALERT Hardware Authority Retained

ADS1115 Board 1 ALERT Continues To Operate Through
The AITRIP 5V Opto-Isolated Relay Module.

The AITRIP Relay Interrupts Positive Contactor Coil
Authorization During Hardware Fault Conditions.

The ADS1115 ALERT Output Does Not Directly Drive
The Contactor Coil.

Affected Specifications

04_ADS1115_Analog_Front_End.txt

08_Protection_And_Fault_Response.txt

---

Developmental Perfboard Connector Pin Assignments Retired

Connector Pin Numbers Used On Developmental Perfboards
Are Not Considered Permanent SNAPACK Connector Assignments.

The Current Development Boards Will Not Be Installed
As The Final SNAPACK Hardware.

Final Connector And PCB Pin Assignments Shall Be
Established During Manufactured PCB Development.

No Final PCB Pin Numbers Are Assigned At This Revision.

Affected Specifications

01_Authoritative_Pin_Mapping.txt
