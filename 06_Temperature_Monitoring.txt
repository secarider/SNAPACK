============================================================
ONEWIRE BUS
============================================================

All Five DS18B20 Sensors Share One Common
OneWire Data Line.

The Data Line Connects To The Display Through
The Assigned Temperature Data Connection.

All Sensor Grounds Connect To System Ground.

All Sensor VCC Wires Connect To The Regulated
3.3V Rail.

============================================================
PULL-UP RESISTOR
============================================================

One Master Pull-Up Resistor Is Used For The
Entire OneWire Bus.

Value

4.7kΩ

Connection

Regulated 3.3V Rail
        |
      4.7kΩ
        |
OneWire Data Line

============================================================
MECHANICAL INSTALLATION
============================================================

Temperature Probes Shall Be Mounted At The
Specified Cable-To-Bus And Bus Connection Areas.

Probe Placement May Be Adjusted Slightly To
Accommodate Final Mechanical Fit While Preserving
The Intended Measurement Location.

Probes Shall Be Secured Against Movement.

Sensor Wiring Shall Be Routed Away From High
Current Conductors Where Practical.

============================================================
MEASUREMENT PURPOSE
============================================================

Temperature Measurements Support

• Normal Temperature Display

• Individual Sensor Identification

• High Temperature Protection

• Temperature Agreement Monitoring

• Cold Charge Protection

• Event Logging
