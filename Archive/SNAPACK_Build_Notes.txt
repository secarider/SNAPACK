SNAPACK BUILD NOTES AND THOUGHTS
================================

PURPOSE
-------
Archive practical construction notes, bench observations, build choices,
unfinished ideas, and superseded methods found across the old Hardware
Manifest, revised Hardware Manifest, Software Manifest, and Operational
Specification.

This is NOT the authoritative pin map or wiring specification. The revised
Hardware Manifest and 01_Pin_Maps_Authoritative.txt take precedence for the
current physical build.

Historical notes are deliberately retained. "Archived" or "superseded"
means the note explains an earlier physical build or design path; it does
not mean that method should be used now.


============================================================
CURRENT / USEFUL BUILD NOTES
============================================================

GENERAL PHYSICAL BUILD
----------------------
- Main high-current positive path remains uninterrupted; high-current
  switching is on the negative side.
- Low-current control and terminating connections are intended to live in
  the service-hatch area where practical.
- LEV100 mounting plan: four contactors in the left wing and four in the
  right wing, with 3/16-inch 110 copper bus bars.
- EV200 contactors are a parallel pair for the 5S / ~16V path.
- LEV100 contactors form the 4S / ~12V path.
- Use female connectors on live/source-side connections where practical;
  male connectors on passive-side connections.
- Wire numbers are a working physical-wire plan and should be confirmed
  against the actual mockup.

RELAY SOCKETS / FLYBACK
-----------------------
- Automotive relay terminal 86+ is positive coil supply; 85- is
  negative/control return.
- 1N4007 flyback diode stripe faces terminal 86+.
- Relay sockets were de-pinned at 85 and 86 and diode leads soldered
  directly to the wire crimps.
- Five sockets were originally prepared. Four remain used by the current
  automotive-relay design. The fifth gatekeeper socket was replaced by
  the 5V opto-isolated relay module.

BREAK-BEFORE-MAKE CONTACTOR INTERLOCK
-------------------------------------
- Physical relay interlock prevents the 12V LEV100 bank and 16V EV200
  bank from being intentionally energized together.
- Relay transit time is deliberately used in both directions to create a
  break-before-make interval.
- Original working estimate: roughly 50-100 ms.
- Bench mockup should measure the actual transition timing using the
  final-output sense channels rather than relying on the estimate.

PROCESSOR LOCKOUT / OPTO RELAY
------------------------------
- ADS1115 ALERT must not directly carry an automotive relay-coil load.
- Current interface: AITRIP 5V one-channel opto-isolated relay module,
  ASIN B096M77HCJ, with onboard driver and flyback protection.
- Relay contacts interrupt the positive contactor-authorization path.
- Authorized state allows operation without requiring the processor to
  continuously hold an automotive relay energized.
- This replaces the earlier automotive gatekeeper-relay concept.

OTC 3500-01 CURRENT SENSOR
--------------------------
- OTC clamp fixed on 2000A range.
- Working scale: 1mV = 1A.
- Thumbwheel was zeroed by meter and immobilized with a small hot-glue
  dollop; software zeroing remains possible.
- Intended internal installation is on the positive side.
- OEM clamp ribbon was extended using a 9-pin JST arrangement: eight
  factory conductors plus one shield/drain.
- Shield/drain terminates to system ground and is not a signal conductor.
- OTC PCB was cleaned, conformal-coated, and ribbon strain-relieved.
- Extension permits the electronics PCB to live farther from the clamp.
- Clamp orientation is to be confirmed during mockup; software polarity
  inversion can compensate if installation requires reversal.
- Isolated supply negative and analog signal negative join at the clamp,
  allowing a three-wire run.
- A shielded 3- or 4-wire scrap cable was considered suitable; an old USB
  cable was specifically noted as a possible donor.

OTC ISOLATED POWER SUPPLY
-------------------------
- Power chain: 12V -> waterproof 5V buck -> B0509S-1WR3 isolated 9V
  converter.
- B0509S-1WR3 assembly mounts on the perfboard with the analog front end.
- Waterproof 5V buck is a separate potted module; it does not need to be
  mounted on that perfboard or conformal-coated.
- B0509S-1WR3 PCB and solder joints may be conformal-coated.
- 4.7uF polarized capacitor across the 5V input, close to converter pins.
- 2.2uF polarized capacitor across isolated 9V output, close to pins.
- One 100nF ceramic capacitor parallels each polarized capacitor.
- Tight-quarters note: small ceramic can be attached to the larger
  capacitor first, then the pair mounted using the larger capacitor legs.
- Isolated 9V output is dedicated to the OTC electronics.

The B0509S-1WR3 isolated 5V → 9V converter requires a minimum output load
for operation within its specified performance range.

Bench testing confirmed successful 5V → 9V operation with two 1 kΩ resistors
connected in parallel across the isolated 9V output.

Permanent preload:

* Resistor 1: 1 kΩ across 9V output
* Resistor 2: 1 kΩ across 9V output
* Effective resistance: approximately 500 Ω
* Preload current at 9V: approximately 18 mA
* Dissipation per 1 kΩ resistor: approximately 0.081 W

The two 1 kΩ resistors shall remain installed as a permanent minimum-load
preload rather than being treated as temporary test components.

This preload is included in the converter's total output load and therefore
counts toward the B0509S-1WR3 maximum output-current capability of 111ma.

ADS1115 / ANALOG FRONT END
--------------------------
- Two ADS1115 modules are mounted on the perfboard.
- Current channel allocation:
    Board 1 A0 = B2 cumulative voltage
    Board 1 A1 = B3 cumulative voltage
    Board 1 A2 = B4 cumulative voltage
    Board 1 A3 = B5 cumulative voltage
    Board 2 A0 = B1 cumulative voltage
    Board 2 A1 = final output positive
    Board 2 A2 = OTC current
    Board 2 A3 = final output negative
- Board 1 ADDR: tiny jumper directly to system ground = 0x48.
- Board 2 ADDR: tiny jumper directly to +5V VDD = 0x49.
- Seven protected voltage-sense channels use the same
  47k / 18k / 100nF / P6KE18A circuit.
- Final-output sense wires originate at the last internal high-current
  positive and negative connections before the jump cables leave the pack.
- Build note calls for substantial sense wiring and says not to obtain
  these measurements from another location.
- Five cumulative battery-tap channels retain their isolation relay so
  divider resistors are disconnected when the pack is off.

P6KE18A TVS INSTALLATION
------------------------
- Painted stripe faces the positive/raw sense line.
- Other end terminates at system ground.
- On the five BMS tap channels, TVS devices remain inboard of the
  voltage-divider isolation relay.
- Current implementation uses seven: five cumulative taps plus final
  output positive and final output negative.

3.3V REGULATED SUPPLY
---------------------
- AMS1117-3.3 module is fed from the regulated 5V rail.
- Regulated 3.3V supplies the level-shifter LV reference, all five DS18B20
  sensors, OneWire 4.7k pull-up, and suitable future 3.3V GPIO pull-ups.
- The old resistor-divider-derived 3.3V node is retired as a supply rail.

LOGIC LEVEL SHIFTER
-------------------
- HV reference = regulated 5V.
- LV reference = regulated 3.3V from AMS1117-3.3.
- Grounds tie to common system ground.
- Current physical orientation:
    SCL = HV4 <-> LV4
    SDA = HV3 <-> LV3
- Channels 1 and 2 remain empty as spares.
- A signal must stay on the same numbered channel across the shifter.

TEMPERATURE BUS
---------------
- Five DS18B20 sensors share one OneWire data bus.
- One 4.7k pull-up connects DATA to regulated 3.3V.
- DATA leaves through output-header pin 8 and continues to display-ribbon
  pin 6 / TX.
- The five sensor names/locations identify physical probes, not five
  separate OneWire data conductors.

HEADERS / SERVICEABILITY
------------------------
- B0509S-1WR3 5V input and isolated 9V output were duplicated onto
  individual two-pin perfboard headers.
- Original corresponding 10-pin-input-header positions remain electrically
  active, but dedicated two-pin headers are used for these supplies.
- Remaining six input positions form the standardized 6S BMS cable
  connection for B0-B5.
- Display ribbon pin numbers are the silkscreen numbers on the back of the
  Elecrow display PCB; the breakout board does not get a second numbering
  scheme.

MOCKUP / VERIFICATION
---------------------
- Measure actual break-before-make transition timing.
- Confirm OTC clamp orientation and current polarity.
- Verify every physical wire number against actual source, destination,
  connector and pin.
- Record wire color and gauge where useful.
- Remove planned wire-index entries that do not physically exist.
- Use final-output voltage plus OTC current for loaded-voltage sag and
  practical pack-path resistance testing.
- 01_Pin_Maps_Authoritative.txt wins if any pin conflict is found.

SOFTWARE / DISPLAY BUILD THOUGHTS
---------------------------------
- Final-output +/- sensing supports actual cable-to-cable output voltage,
  12V/16V confirmation, loaded sag and pack-path resistance estimation.
- Pack-path resistance is a practical load-event trend, not laboratory AC
  impedance measurement.
- Normal temperature display summarizes the five sensors; abnormal spread
  should identify the named abnormal/hottest sensor.
- Low-temperature charge protection uses the lowest sensor for charging
  eligibility while allowing discharge.
- Existing Elecrow demo/navigation behavior should be retained where
  practical rather than replacing the UI foundation unnecessarily.
- Animation is secondary to monitoring reliability and should be reduced
  or disabled if it interferes with sampling or display updates.


============================================================
HISTORICAL / SUPERSEDED BUILD NOTES
============================================================

RESISTOR-DIVIDER 3.3V SUPPLY — OBSOLETE
---------------------------------------
Earlier physical build:
- 5V rail feeding a resistor divider to produce approximately 3.3V.
- 10k upper resistor.
- Two 10k resistors in series as the lower leg to system ground.
- Center junction used as the nominal 3.3V node.
- 4.7k OneWire pull-up soldered directly to that node.
- Temperature-sensor VCC and level-shifter LV taken from that node.
- Some construction notes describe the 4.7k resistor as "mid-air" mounted.

This was replaced by the AMS1117-3.3 regulated supply. Retained here to
explain the earlier build, photographs, and old manifest references.

DIRECT ADS1115 ALERT -> AUTOMOTIVE RELAY COIL — SUPERSEDED
----------------------------------------------------------
Earlier design:
- +12V directly to positive side of an automotive authorization-relay coil.
- ADS1115 ALERT directly to the negative coil side.
- Flyback diode across the coil.
- Relaxed SPDT state = authorization allowed.
- ALERT grounding the coil = authorization interrupted.

This was replaced by the AITRIP 5V opto-isolated relay module so the
ADS1115 ALERT output no longer carries automotive relay-coil current.

OLD OUTPUT-HEADER ALLOCATION — SUPERSEDED
-----------------------------------------
Older notes assigned final-output sense to pins 8 and 9 and left pin 10
reserved. Other older notes also reflect the historical off-by-one header
identification problem.

Current mapping:
- Pin 8  = temperature sensor DATA
- Pin 9  = final output positive sense
- Pin 10 = final output negative sense

The authoritative pin-map file supersedes all archived mappings.

OLD LEVEL-SHIFTER CHANNELS — SUPERSEDED
---------------------------------------
Earlier notes used LV1/HV1 and LV2/HV2 for SCL/SDA. The rebuilt physical
layout required the shifter module to be flipped for better fit.

Current:
- SCL = LV4/HV4
- SDA = LV3/HV3
- Channels 1 and 2 = spares.

OLD ADS1115 BOARD-1 ADDRESS NOTE — SUPERSEDED
--------------------------------------------
An earlier build left Board 1 ADDR floating and observed 0x48, described
as essentially "no harm no foul."

Current build intentionally jumpers:
- Board 1 ADDR -> system ground = 0x48
- Board 2 ADDR -> +5V VDD = 0x49

ORIGINAL SIX-CHANNEL ANALOG FRONT END — SUPERSEDED
--------------------------------------------------
Original implementation used five cumulative pack-voltage channels plus
one OTC current channel, leaving two ADS1115 inputs unused.

Current design uses seven protected voltage dividers plus OTC, consuming
all eight ADS1115 channels. The two added dividers measure final output
positive and final output negative.

EARLY OTC DISASSEMBLY IDEA
--------------------------
An early note proposed removing the OTC outer case and retaining only the
functional electronics, with conformal coating on exposed circuitry.

Later build notes record the more specific physical implementation:
extended OEM ribbon, relocated electronics PCB, cleaned/conformal-coated
PCB, strain relief, and permanent isolated 9V supply.
