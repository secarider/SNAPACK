=========================================================
            CONTACTOR INTERLOCK / BREAK-BEFORE-MAKE
=========================================================
Purpose:
Guarantees The LEV 100 Banks 12v Contactor Releases Before
The 16v EV200 Main Boost Path Contactor Is Allowed To Energize.
Must Work For Both Transition To And Detransition From 16v Boost Function

Theory Of Operation:
1 SPDT 12v In Its Relaxed (Unpowered) State Turns The 12v Lev100 Banks Control Relay To Authorized,
Allowing The Timer Switch Signals To Reach The Controls.
The Open Pole Of Same Double Throw Relay Assures 16v EV200 Contactor Is NOT Authorized. 
This Is Probably Sufficient As A Safety Interlock To Insure Either Contactor Bank Can Never Be On At The Same Time,
However,
There Is Concern For The Amount Of Amps Being Pushed At The Moment Of Commanded Contactor 
Switchover Could Pose A Risk (Even If Small) Of The 2 Voltages Running Into Each Other.
Which Could Weld The Contacts Together Microseconds After The Commanded Switchover.
So It Should Be Simple Enough To Introduce A Controlled Delay Long Enough To Guarantee 
12v Contactor Has Released Before The 16v Contactor Can Engage.
The Resultant Burn Down Of A 5p5s Pack From Such An Unlikely But Not Zero Chance Event Could Be Quite Energetic
To Say The Least, So Extra Precautions Are Being Employed By Stretching Out
The Sequence Using 2 Relays To Double The Time Alotted For Contactor Disconnect Before Connect 
" Break For Sure Before Make " And Still Accomplish The Switchover In No Less >50ms No More Than 100 milliseconds
And Works For Both Transition To And Detransition From 16v Boost Function.

Function:
12V OFF
    ↓
Delay By Dual Consecutive Relay, NOT DBL Throw, 
An Actual 2nd Relay For Pole/Pivot Transit Travel Time x2 
Best Guess This Will Give Us >50ms Delay, 
This Provides The Same Delay In Both Directions.
Hopefully Preventing Pack Shorting By Contact Overlap 
Resulting In Welded Contactors From An Accidental Make-Before-Break. 
Unlikely, But Not Zero Chance So... Extra Precautions Are Being Employed.
    ↓
16V ON

Location:
Service Hatch

The 12V Path Logic: 
Run The Main Control Power Into Relay 2, Pin 30. 
Solder A Wire From Its Normally Closed Contact (Pin 87a) Over To Relay 1, Pin 30. 
Finally, Run Relay 1'S Normally Open Contact (Pin 87) Out To The LEV100 Contactor Coils.

The 16V Path Logic: 
Mirror The Exact Same Step On The Other Side. Run Main Control Power Into Relay 1, Pin 30. 
Solder A Wire From Its Normally Closed Contact (Pin 87a) Over To Relay 2, Pin 30. 
Then, Run Relay 2'S Normally Open Contact (Pin 87) Out To The Big EV200 Contactor Coils.

### B0509S-1WR3 Permanent Minimum Load

The B0509S-1WR3 isolated 5V → 9V converter requires a minimum output load
for operation within its specified performance range.
Bench testing confirmed successful 5V → 9V operation with two 1 kΩ resistors
connected in parallel across the isolated 9V output.

Permanent preload:

* Resistor 1: 1 kΩ across 9V output
* Resistor 2: 1 kΩ across 9V output
* Effective resistance: approximately 500 Ω
* Preload current at 9V: approximately 18 mA
* Dissipation per 1 kΩ resistor: approximately 0.081 W

The two 1 kΩ resistors shall remain installed as a permanent minimum-load
preload rather than being treated as temporary test components.

This preload is included in the converter's total output load and therefore
counts toward the B0509S-1WR3 maximum output-current capability of 111ma.

The OTC circuitry and permanent preload combined shall remain within the
converter's rated output-current capability of 111ma.

            PROTECTION
=========================================================
The P6KE18A Is A Transient Voltage Suppressor (TVS) Diode. 
Under Normal Conditions, It Sits Completely Dormant And Invisible. 
However, If A Massive Voltage Spike 
(Like A Starter Motor Inductive Kickback Or A Surface Charge Surge) 
Hits The System, It Snaps Alive Instantly To Clamp And Shunt That Energy Safely 
To Ground Before It Can Travel Down The Small Signal Wires
Because The 5S Full Pack Max Voltage Is 18.25V, 
We Have Selected An 18V TVS To Act As A Threshold Guard.
On The Divider Board Right Before The Signal Hits The Resistors. 
They Belong Directly Across Each Raw Incoming Protected Voltage Sense Line:
The Five Cumulative Battery Taps Plus Final Output Positive And Final Output Negative.
Stripe To Positive With All Of Their Ends At System Ground.

The Power Reference: 
The Shifter Needs To Know What Both "High" And "Low" Mean. 
Connect 3.3V+ To The LV Pin, And Connect The Main 5V+ Rail To The HV Pin.

Common Ground: Tie The GND Pins On Both Sides Of The Shifter To Pack Ground/Negative.

Board 1: 
(B2 Through B5 Cumulative Pack Voltage Taps):
Solder A Tiny Jumper Wire From The ADDR Pin Straight To The SYS GND Pin On This Board. 
This Names It Address 0x48

Board 2: 
(B1 + Final Output Positive + OTC Current + Final Output Negative): 
Solder A Tiny Jumper Wire From The ADDR Pin Straight To The 5v+ VDD Pin On This Board. 
This Names It Address 0x49

The Data Path: 
SDA And SCL From The Twin ADS1115 Boards Are Tied Together Then They Go Into HV3 And HV4. 
They Exit Cleanly Through LV3 And LV4 Straight To The Elecrow Display

The Power References: 
HV Gets The Clean 5V+ Rail (From The Buck Converter), 
LV Gets Regulated 3.3V+ From The AMS1117-3.3 Module

The Ground Plane: 
Both Grounds On The Shifter Tie Together Right Into The Central System Ground

The Spares: 
Channels 1 And 2 (LV1, LV2, HV1, HV2) Stay Completely Empty And Unpopulated, 
Serving As Perfect Backups If A Channel Ever Gets Cooked 

Direct Channel Passthrough: 
Align The Data Lines Perfectly. 
If Clock (SCL) Goes Into LV4, It Must Exit Through HV4 To Go To The ADS1115 Boards. 
If  Data (SDA) Goes Into LV3, It Must Exit Through HV3 To Go To The ADS1115 Boards.
Dont Forget To Set The Address Of The Boards Before You Stack Them.

The ADS1115 ALERT Output Does Not Carry Relay-Coil Current.

Pin 4 Is System Ground.
This Ties The System Ground Rail To 12-Pin Ribbon Pins 11 And 12.

Pin 5 Is 5V Power.
This Connects The Main 5V Power Rail To 12-Pin Ribbon Pins 1 And 2
For The Elecrow Display.

Pin 6 Is SCL 3.3V Logic.
This Connects Directly To The LV4 Pin On The Low Side Of The Logic Level Shifter
And Routes To 12-Pin Ribbon Pin 8 SCL.

Pin 7 Is SDA 3.3V Logic.
This Connects Directly To The LV3 Pin On The Low Side Of The Logic Level Shifter
And Routes To 12-Pin Ribbon Pin 7 SDA.

Pin 8 Is Temperature Sensor Data.
This Connects To The 4.7k Ohm Master Pull-Up Data Node And The Five
Temperature Sensor Yellow Wires.
It Routes To 12-Pin Ribbon Pin 6 TX.

Pin 9 Is Final Output Voltage Sense (+).
This Routes Through The Protected Divider Network To ADS1115 Board 2 A1.

Pin 10 Is Final Output Voltage Sense (-).
This Routes Through The Protected Divider Network To ADS1115 Board 2 A3.

==========================================================
TEMPERATURE SENSORS PULL UP RESISTOR MID AIR MOUNTING
==========================================================

Step 1 Locate The Regulated 3.3V Output From The AMS1117-3.3 Module.

Step 2 Take The 4.7k Ohm Temperature Master Pull Up Resistor
(Yellow Violet Red Gold Bands) And Solder One Leg To The Regulated 3.3V Rail.

Step 3 Connect All 5 Temperature Sensor Red VCC Wires To The Same
Regulated 3.3V Rail.

Step 4 Take The Remaining Leg Of The 4.7k Ohm Pull-Up Resistor
And Connect It To Output Header Pin 8. This Is The Temperature Data Line.

Step 5 Connect All 5 Temperature Sensor Yellow Data Wires To That
Same Output Header Pin 8 Data Node.

Step 6 Connect All 5 Temperature Sensor Black Ground Wires To System Ground.

Output Header Pin 8 Routes The Data Line To 12-Pin Ribbon Pin 6 (TX).
