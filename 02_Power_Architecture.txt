SNAPACK Hardware Specification

Revision: Draft 1

============================================================
PURPOSE
============================================================

This Specification Defines The SNAPACK Power Distribution
Architecture.

It Documents The Battery Configuration, Power Conversion,
Voltage Rails, Isolation Boundaries, And Distribution Of
Electrical Power Throughout The System.

Connector Pin Assignments Are Defined Separately In:

    01_Authoritative_Pin_Mapping.txt

============================================================
BATTERY ARCHITECTURE
============================================================

Battery Chemistry

    LiFePO4

Cell Type

    Headway 38120HP

Configuration

    Five Cells In Series
    Five Cells In Parallel

    (5S5P)

Nominal Pack Voltage

    Approximately 16 Volts

The Midpoint Of The Pack Provides The Normal 12-Volt Output.

The Full Pack Provides The 16-Volt Boost Output.

============================================================
PRIMARY POWER RAILS
============================================================

The SNAPACK Contains The Following Power Rails:

• Full Pack Voltage

• 12-Volt Operating Rail

• Regulated 5-Volt Rail

• Regulated 3.3-Volt Logic Rail

• Isolated +9V Supply

• Isolated -9V Supply

============================================================
POWER CONVERSION
============================================================

5-Volt Rail

The Primary 5-Volt Rail Powers:

• Display Assembly

• ADS1115 Modules

• Relay Module

• AMS1117 Regulator

• Other 5V Peripheral Devices

------------------------------------------------------------

3.3-Volt Rail

The System 3.3V Rail Is Generated By An AMS1117-3.3
Voltage Regulator Module Powered From The Existing
5V Rail.

The 3.3V Rail Powers:

• Logic Level Shifter

• DS18B20 Temperature Sensors

• OneWire Pull-Up Resistor

• Future 3.3V Logic Inputs

------------------------------------------------------------

Isolated Supplies

A B0509S Isolated DC-DC Converter Produces

• +9V Isolated

• -9V Isolated

These Supplies Are Used Only By The OTC Current Sensor
Front-End.

No Other Circuit Shall Share The Isolated Ground.

============================================================
POWER DISTRIBUTION
============================================================

Battery Power Is Distributed According To Function.

High Current Loads

• EV200 Contactors

• LEV100 Contactors

• Output Terminals

Medium Current Loads

• DC-DC Converters

• Relay Coil Circuits

Low Power Electronics

• Display

• ADS1115 Boards

• Logic Level Shifter

• Temperature Sensors

• Relay Module

============================================================
GROUND SYSTEMS
============================================================

The SNAPACK Utilizes Two Independent Ground Systems.

------------------------------------------------------------

System Ground

Common Ground Shared By:

• Battery Pack

• Display

• ADS1115 Boards

• Relay Module

• Temperature Sensors

• Logic Electronics

------------------------------------------------------------

Isolated Ground

Dedicated Ground Used Only By:

• OTC Current Sensor Circuit

• B0509S Isolated Output

The Isolated Ground Shall Not Be Connected To System
Ground Except Through The Intended Current Sensor
Measurement Circuit.

============================================================
DESIGN REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

The 5V And 3.3V Rails Shall Remain Stable During
Contactor Switching Events.

Logic Electronics Shall Never Operate Directly From
Battery Voltage.

The AMS1117 Shall Be The Only Source Of Regulated
3.3V Within The System.

All Power Conversion Modules Shall Be Mounted To
Permit Replacement Without Redesign Of Adjacent
Subsystems.

============================================================
VERIFICATION
============================================================

Verify:

□ 5V Rail

□ 3.3V Rail

□ +9V Isolated Rail

□ -9V Isolated Rail

□ Isolation Between System Ground And Isolated Ground

□ Correct Voltage At Each Distribution Point

□ No Excessive Regulator Heating
